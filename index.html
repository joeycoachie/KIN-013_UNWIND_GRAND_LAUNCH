UNWIND PROTOCOL

SYSTEMIC DECOMPRESSION

1. The Barber Pole

Execution

Anchor bilateral ischial tuberosities. Rotate above T12 to wring the ribcage.

Safety Gate

Opposite hip lifts to twist (False range)[cite: 5].

RA OVERRIDE

Perform seated on a stability chair with back support to maintain spinal integrity without deep unsupported rotation.

2. Climbing The Trunk

Execution

Segmental extension. Active dorsiflexion. Climb hands up the leg[cite: 5].

Safety Gate

Hinging backward with flat back[cite: 5].

5. Pearls Off The Mat

Execution

Safety Gate
